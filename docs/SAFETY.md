# Development Safety Boundary

BanglaMedScribe V1 is a clinical documentation prototype, not an autonomous diagnostic or prescribing system.

During the showcase/pre-pilot phase:

- use synthetic, scripted, public, or appropriately consented audio;
- do not upload uncontrolled real patient data;
- do not present model output as a final medical record without clinician review;
- do not infer or prescribe treatments autonomously;
- do not silently convert model inference into a clinician statement;
- treat medication names, dosages, frequencies, negation, and numeric values as safety-critical information.

Privacy, retention, access controls, and clinical pilot governance must be implemented and reviewed before real-patient deployment.
